Questions we hear first.

What do I need for a commercial auto quote?

A USDOT or MC number if you have one, driver details, vehicle year, make, and model or VINs, where you operate, what you haul or how you use the vehicles, and a current policy or loss history if you have it.

Do you insure new authorities?

Tell us about the operation. We’ll look for carriers that fit. We don’t promise a market before we see the details.

Can you help with USDOT-related insurance filings?

We help you understand which filings an operation needs and work with the carrier on submitting them. What applies depends on the authority and the states you run. We confirm that before anything is sent.

How do I get a certificate of insurance?

Call, text, or email the certificate holder’s name and address, and any wording they require. We’ll tell you what we need to request it.

Can I add or remove a truck mid-policy?

Usually, yes. Contact us before the change so the policy, and any filing tied to it, can be updated.

Do you offer personal car insurance too?

Yes. Use the personal auto section on this page, or call or text 516-820-0277.
